# Hi, I'm Folorunso Quadri Ayobami 👋

**Computational drug discovery scientist** with 3+ years of experience applying computational tools, machine learning and AI to drug discovery and design. Pharmacy background (Obafemi Awolowo University, Nigeria).

## 🔬 What I do
- Structure-based design: molecular docking, MM-GBSA binding energy, pharmacophore modelling
- Ligand-based design: QSAR modelling, virtual screening
- ADMET and drug-likeness evaluation (Lipinski, QED, PAINS, synthetic accessibility)
- Machine learning and generative AI for de novo molecular design

## 🧪 Projects

### Multi-objective de novo design and virtual screening against Lassa virus nucleoprotein (3MX5)
Project for the Lassa fever drug discovery and design challenge in Nigeria. Combined virtual screening of a phytochemical library with REINVENT4 generative modelling to design drug-like inhibitors of the Lassa virus nucleoprotein.
- Multi-objective filtering: Lipinski's Rule of Five, PAINS, QED, synthetic accessibility, Tanimoto similarity
- Chemical space analytics to quantify library diversity
- Generative design with REINVENT4, followed by molecular docking of top candidates
- *Repository: coming soon*

### De novo design of acetylcholinesterase inhibitors for Alzheimer's disease
Generative design of novel acetylcholinesterase (AChE) inhibitors using **REINVENT4**, followed by multi-model machine learning classification and structure-based virtual screening to prioritise candidates.
- Generative modelling: REINVENT4
- Multi-model ML classification of active vs inactive compounds
- Structure-based virtual screening and docking
- *Repository: coming soon*

### Computational evaluation of phytochemicals and FDA-approved antiretrovirals against HIV-1 protease (PDB: 1HSG)
Compared natural-product phytochemicals with approved antiretroviral drugs (atazanavir, darunavir) as potential HIV protease inhibitors, as safer alternatives to drugs limited by side effects and resistance.
- Molecular docking and MM-GBSA binding free energy calculations (Schrödinger Suite / Maestro)
- Pharmacokinetic and drug-likeness profiling: Lipinski's Rule of Five, oral absorption, permeability, toxicity
- *Repository: coming soon*

### Identification of potential novel anti-tuberculosis drug candidates from phytoconstituents of *Azadirachta indica*, *Piper nigrum*, *Garcinia mangostana*, *Allium sativum* and *Ocimum sanctum*: an in-silico study
